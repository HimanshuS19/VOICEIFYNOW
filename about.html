About the project

Speech, made accessible.

VoiceifyNow converts written text into natural-sounding speech, so reading becomes listening — for anyone who needs or prefers it that way.

What we're building

VoiceifyNow is a text-to-speech tool that turns articles, notes, and everyday phrases into spoken audio. With multiple voices and languages to choose from, it helps people listen to text-based content in a more natural and accessible way — whether that's for visual accessibility, pronunciation practice, or simply multitasking.

Features

What's included

Voices

Multiple natural-sounding voices, pulled straight from your browser's speech engine.

Languages

Translate text on the fly and hear it read aloud in eight languages.

Real-time

Speech starts as soon as you press play — no waiting on a server round-trip.

Downloadable

Save a spoken phrase as an audio file to use outside the browser.

Why it helps

Benefits

Accessibility

Makes written content usable for people with visual impairments or reading difficulties.

Learning

Helps language learners and students hear correct pronunciation and improve comprehension.

Time back

Listen to text-based content while doing something else entirely.

Your voice

Pick the voice, language, and pace that fits how you like to listen.
